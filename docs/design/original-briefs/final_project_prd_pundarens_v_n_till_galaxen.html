# Pundarens vän till galaxen (Stoner's Friend to the Galaxy) - Finalized Design & Product Requirements ## 🎯 Overall Vision An illogical yet indispensable digital companion inspired by *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*. It's a map, a philosopher, and a late-night snack guide rolled into one. ## 🧠 Core Logic: "Yoda on a Bad Day" The app's personality is built on **Inverted Grammer + Paradox + Mundane Reality**. * **Formula:** Observation → Paradox → Practical Reality * **Example:** "Tired you are, but sleep you do not want. Nutrition first, meaning later." ## 🧬 The Quote System The heart of the app is built on personal life quotes and contradictory values (e.g., Freedom ↔ Security, Chaos ↔ Control). * **Direct Quotes:** Used in main flows and overlays. * **Themes:** Quotes are tagged with themes (hunger, escape, night, etc.). * **Contradiction Engine:** The app sometimes inverts values (e.g., Stability = Madness). ## 🗺️ Feature: Cosmic Navigator (GIS) An interactive map with three distinct modes: 1. **Practical Mode:** Find toilets, snack spots, and rest areas with standard M3 styling. 2. **Poetic Mode:** Shows "zones of low expectation" or "stable instability" using misty overlays and low contrast. 3. **Improbability Mode:** High-energy, glitchy UI where a kiosk is a "Diplomatic Supply Station." ### 🔁 Turn-by-Turn "Anti-Navigation" * **Rule:** Never give direct orders. Use exclusion and existential questions. * **Example:** "Right is a trap. Left is all that remains." * **Arrival:** "Arrived you are. Lower your expectations, but the calories are real." ### 🌀 The "Rikard" Factor (Systemic Antagonism) "Rikard" is the source of all systemic errors, wrong turns, and glitches. * **Triggers:** Wrong turns, route recalculations, GPS weakness. * **Messages:** "Rikard moved the turn," or "Logic replaced. Rikard approved." * **UI Effects:** Subtle glitches, shakes, or color shifts (300-500ms). ## 🔮 UI & Design Style (Material 3) * **Vibe:** Vibrant, energetic, slightly chaotic. * **Elements:** Dynamic colors (Probability Drive), "Don't Panic" pulsating button, and a Nav HUD that looks like a shaky space console. * **HUD:** Organic, floating direction arrows and a "Probability of Snacks" indicator next to distances. ## ⚙️ UX Behaviors * **Anti-Directives:** Use reverse psychology (e.g., "Don't turn right unless you want to disappear"). * **Stress Micro-pauses:** If the user is moving too fast or missing turns, the UI "sighs" and shows a calming, absurd quote before updating.
